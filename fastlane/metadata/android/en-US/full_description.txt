GPS Car Tracking records GPS tracks enriched with live OBD-II metrics from an ELM327 adapter (Bluetooth LE GATT or Classic SPP), queues samples on the device, and uploads them in batches to a backend you control.

FEATURES
• Foreground tracking with platform location (system fused on Android 12+, GPS on older devices)
• Native OBD over BLE or Classic Bluetooth — no proprietary Google Play location libraries
• ECU-aware sessions: tracking can follow adapter connectivity
• Durable offline queue (Room) with batch upload and retry
• Fuel and vehicle settings for estimated consumption from OBD signals
• Dashboard gauges, settings (including QR provisioning), and OBD debug log
• No ads, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting trackers baked in

BACKEND (YOU CHOOSE)
• Prefer self-hosting the free/open companion platform:
  https://github.com/lfdominguez/my-car-tracking-platform
• Or use any wire-compatible HTTP API (/start, /stop, /samples) with a device token
• Optional community-hosted instance: https://mycar.domivega.com
  (configure URLs and token in Settings; nothing is hard-required at build time)

PERMISSIONS (WHY)
• Location (including background while tracking) — write track points
• Bluetooth / nearby devices — talk to the ELM327 dongle
• Notifications & foreground service — keep a trip alive reliably
• Camera (optional) — scan provisioning QR codes from your web console
• Internet — upload queued samples when you configure a server
• Boot completed — re-arm lightweight auto-connect / presence after reboot

PRIVACY
Samples and credentials stay on your phone until you point the app at a server you trust. Defaults ship empty / placeholder hostnames. BLE addresses and API tokens are stored only in app settings on device.
